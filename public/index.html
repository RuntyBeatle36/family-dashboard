        🎨 Graphics

        Glass Blur Effect

        The frosted-glass look on the top bar, calendar, and popups. Performance: this is one of the single most GPU-intensive effects in the app on Raspberry Pi. Every blurred panel has to re-blur whatever's behind it (including the animated weather), every frame it changes. Reduced uses a much cheaper blur radius, most of the savings, still frosted. Off removes the cost almost entirely; panels switch to a solid color instead of frosted glass, since without any blur the usual near-transparent panels are too see-through to stay readable. Full is the default look.

        Weather Animation

        Controls the animated sky behind the dashboard (rain, snow, clouds, stars). Performance: Off stops the animation loop entirely, the biggest possible saving. Reduced keeps the effect at roughly half the particle count. Full is the default look.

        Lightning Flashes

        The screen-flash-and-bolt effect during Storm weather. Performance: each strike re-renders a jagged bolt shape with several glow layers, a real but brief spike, only during storms. Disabling it keeps the rain and dark sky, minus the flash.
